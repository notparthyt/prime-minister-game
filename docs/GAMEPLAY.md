# Gameplay Guide

## Goal
Lead Valoria (blue) to dominance, or at least survive in office for 15 years.

## Stats
- **Treasury:** gold. Income per quarter = provinces x 4 x tax multiplier + economy x 0.8 + trade deals (8 each) - military x 0.35.
- **Approval:** your popularity. Below 45% at an election you lose. At 0% there is a coup.
- **Military:** your army strength. Costs upkeep every quarter.
- **Economy:** boosts income. Invest in industry to raise it.
- **Land held:** share of all land provinces you own.

## Actions (3 per quarter)
| Action | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Invest in industry | 100 gold | Economy +8 |
| Recruit troops | 80 gold | Military +10 |
| Fund welfare | 90 gold | Approval +10 |
| Send aid | 60 gold | Relations +16 with a nation |
| Trade deal | free | +8 gold per quarter, relations +6 (needs relations 0+) |
| Propose alliance | free | Chance based on relations (needs 35+) |
| Declare war | -3 approval | Allies of both sides may join |
| Offer peace | free | Chance depends on relative strength and enemy aggression |
| Attack province | 10 gold | Must border your land, you must be at war with the owner |

Changing the tax level is free and does not use an action.

## Tax levels
| Level | Income | Approval per quarter |
|---|---|---|
| Low | x0.7 | +2 |
| Normal | x1.0 | 0 |
| High | x1.4 | -3 |

Each war you are fighting costs 1.5 approval per quarter. Approval slowly drifts toward 50-60.

## Combat
Attack power = your military (plus 25% of each ally's) x random 0.8-1.3.
Defense power = defender military (plus allies) x random 0.8-1.3 x 1.1 (x1.5 for a capital).
Winner takes the province. Capturing a capital collapses the nation and every province it owns goes to the conqueror.

## Elections and events
Every 12 quarters there is an election. Roughly 40% of quarters trigger a random event with two choices.

## Tips
1. Ally with a strong neighbour early so it fights alongside you.
2. Build trade deals while at peace; they keep paying for the whole game.
3. Raise military before declaring war, not after.
4. Go for capitals. Taking one is worth a dozen small provinces.
5. Fund welfare before an election, and avoid high taxes in the last year before one.
